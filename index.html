Scoring Methodology

Important: MoneyLens scores are decision-support tools, not financial advice. Projected returns are not guaranteed. Always verify current terms directly with the financial institution before making decisions.

The Money Fit Score (0–100)

Every product receives a score based on five weighted categories. Default weights can be adjusted by your personal preferences and financial goal.

Return / Interest Rate 30%
Risk & Capital Protection 30%
Liquidity 20%
Fees & Requirements 10%
Reliability & Convenience 10%

No Hidden Ranking

We never rank a product higher simply because it has a higher advertised rate. Promotional rates that require strict conditions are penalised in the Fees & Requirements category and clearly flagged with a “Rate Reality Check”.

Dynamic Weighting

When you select “Emergency Fund”, Liquidity and Risk weights increase. When you select “Maximum Return”, the Return weight increases. This is why there is no universal #1 product.
